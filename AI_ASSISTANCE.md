# AI assistance

AdReceipt was built by a five-person team with help from AI coding assistants, including ChatGPT,
Codex, and other editor-integrated tools used by individual contributors.

The tools helped the team review architecture, draft and refactor TypeScript, Solidity, React, and
Graph code, generate test cases, diagnose provider errors, check documentation, and edit public
copy. Team members selected the product direction, controlled credentials and provider accounts,
reviewed changes, authorized external actions, executed the live workflow, and validated the final
behavior.

The live claims are supported by independent evidence rather than assistant output: Sepolia
transactions and logs, the deployed contract, the Graph Studio deployment, Privy provider policy
behavior, CRE CLI simulation output, and repeatable repository tests. No AI-generated voiceover is
planned; the required demo has not been recorded yet and must use a team member's natural voice.
